## Key Content Areas by Page

### Homepage
- **Visitor Goal:** Understand what OSED is
- **Key Message:** "Consistency over intensity. Real transformation takes discipline."
- **Action:** "Learn More" or "Apply"

### About
- **Visitor Goal:** Build trust in founder
- **Key Message:** "Dominic knows what he's doing. I trust this approach."
- **Action:** "Apply for Coaching"

### Coaching
- **Visitor Goal:** Understand what coaching includes
- **Key Message:** "This is how real coaching works. Here's exactly what you'll get."
- **Action:** "Apply for Coaching"

### Testimonials
- **Visitor Goal:** See proof of results
- **Key Message:** "Real people got real results. This actually works."
- **Action:** "Apply for Coaching"

### Contact
- **Visitor Goal:** Start working together
- **Key Message:** "This is how we get started. Tell us about your goals."
- **Action:** "Submit Application"

## Navigation Patterns

- **From Every Page:** Can access any other page via header navigation
- **From Every Page:** Can click "Apply for Coaching" button
- **From Hero Sections:** Can click CTA buttons to next logical step
- **Footer Links:** Quick access to key pages

## SEO Structure

- **Homepage** - Primary keyword: "fitness coaching"
- **About** - Brand keywords: "Dominic Brown", "OSED founder"
- **Coaching** - Service keywords: "online coaching", "nutrition coaching"
- **Testimonials** - Social proof, case study keywords
- **Contact** - Conversion keywords: "apply for coaching"

## Mobile Optimization

- **Navigation:** Hamburger menu on mobile
- **Forms:** Full width, touch-friendly
- **Testimonials:** Single column on mobile
- **CTAs:** Large, tappable buttons
- **Spacing:** Generous padding for readability

---

**Sitemap Summary:** 5 main pages + global navigation = Complete, intuitive user journey from "I want fitness coaching" to "I'm applying for OSED coaching"
